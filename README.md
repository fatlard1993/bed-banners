# Bed Banners

Beds go in the loom and come out with a pattern on the blanket.

## Patterning a bed

Put a dyed bed in the loom's banner slot, a dye beside it and a banner pattern if the design needs one, then take out the bed wearing that pattern. Every pattern the loom offers works, layered as deep as a banner can be.

Dyed beds only: the plain straw bed has no colour and the loom will not take it.

Mine a patterned bed and you get the patterned bed back, ready to place somewhere else. Beds standing side by side keep one pattern each. Broken in Creative, it drops nothing, as any bed would.

## Pandorical

Bed Banners goes on the server with Pandorical, and players need Pandorical on their client to use it properly:

- **With Pandorical**, the pattern is drawn on the blanket itself, in the bed's colour, from the pillow to the foot.
- **Without it**, a player sees a banner of the bed's colour lying flat on the mattress instead, and should not use the loom: picking a pattern with a bed in the slot crashes a client without Pandorical. The bed still sleeps, breaks and moves like any other.
